# Merge Hell Runner 2.0.0

A major upgrade to the campaign, combat, and visual experience.

- Rebuilt worlds with richer scenery, distinct enemies, and multipart bosses.
- Four playable characters, including the unlockable Night-shift Engineer.
- Eight-way aiming, crouch fire, and deeper weapon and drone upgrades.
- Three difficulty levels, longer routes, interactive terrain, and hidden secrets.
- Improved character animation, enemy navigation, combat feedback, and HUD clarity.
- English and Simplified Chinese support, improved checkpoints, and optional music and sound—muted by default.

Plus fixes for running-shot range, drone behavior, collisions, and input handling.
